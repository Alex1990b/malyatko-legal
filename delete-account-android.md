# Delete your “Malyatko” account (Android)

How to delete your account in the “Malyatko” app for Android and what happens to your data.

## In the app

1. Open **Malyatko** and go to **Settings**.
2. In the **Account** section, tap **Delete account**.
3. Confirm. If you signed in a while ago, choose your Google account once more to confirm it is you.

## Without the app

Email **malyatkoapp@gmail.com** from the Google account you use in the app, with the subject **“Delete my account”**. We will delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

## What is deleted

- Your account (the link between the app and your Google account).
- Every child profile you own, with all records, diary entries, moments, photos and other data, from our cloud and from your devices. Children you shared also disappear for the family members you shared them with.
- Your membership in children other people shared with you (their data stays with them).
- Your invite codes.

Deletion from our cloud happens immediately.

## What is kept

- Anonymous analytics and crash data collected by Firebase are kept by Google for a limited time under Firebase’s retention settings. They do not contain the information you entered about your child.
- Google Play keeps its own records of purchases. Deleting the account does **not** cancel a subscription: cancel it in the **Google Play app → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**.

See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-android.md) for details.
