# Privacy Policy for “Malyatko” on Android

_Last updated: November 2, 2026_

This policy applies to the **Android** version of “Malyatko”, distributed through Google Play. The iOS version has its own [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).

## Introduction

“Malyatko” (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a mobile application that helps parents and caregivers track and organize information about their child’s daily activities, development and routines.

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information the app collects, where it is stored, who can see it and how you can delete it. It applies to all users, regardless of the app language.

Data in the Android app is **not shared with the iOS app**: the two versions keep separate data.

---

## Your Account

To use the app you sign in with your **Google account**. We receive your Google account’s name, email address and a unique account identifier from Google. We use them to:

- keep your data in your account, so it is restored when you reinstall the app or use another Android device;
- show your name to family members you share a child with.

We never see or store your Google password.

---

## Information You Enter

You decide what to enter. This may include:

- child profiles: name, date of birth, gender, photo and birth measurements;
- tracking records: sleep, feeding, diapers, growth, temperature, doctor visits, vaccines, illnesses, medicines and comments;
- diary entries, moments, monthly photos, teeth, development skills;
- reminders.

### Where it is stored

- On your device.
- In our cloud database, **Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore)**, located in the **European Union (Belgium)**, while you are signed in, so it can be synchronized and restored.
- Photos are stored in **Google Firebase Cloud Storage**, located in the **United States**.

Reminders are kept only on your device. Doctor visits and vaccinations are added to your device calendar only if you choose to.

Data is protected in transit (TLS) and at rest by Google, and access rules allow only the members of a child’s family to read or change that child’s data.

---

## Family Sharing

You can invite a family member to a child with an invite code. Everyone who joins can see and change that child’s profile, records and photos, and sees the names of the other family members. The child’s owner can remove members at any time, and members can leave. Invite codes expire after 7 days.

---

## AI Insights

If you use **AI Insights** (a Premium feature), the app sends the records of the category and period you selected to our server function on **Google Cloud**, which passes them to **OpenAI** to generate the text. The request contains:

- the records of that category (dates, times, amounts, durations and their comments) and, for feeding, related feeding and weight records;
- the child’s age (in days and months) and gender;
- your measurement units and app language.

It does **not** contain the child’s name, photos, your name or your email. The answer is shown to you and is not stored by us. Under OpenAI’s API terms, data sent through the API is not used to train its models and may be retained by OpenAI for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring. Insights are only generated when you tap the button.

---

## Analytics and Crash Reports

We use **Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics** (Google) to understand how the app is used and to fix errors. They collect:

- screen views and actions, such as adding or deleting a record;
- device and app information (model, OS version, app version) and crash reports;
- an app-instance identifier.

We **do not send** child names, dates of birth, photos, health measurements, medical records, comments or any other content you enter to analytics or crash reporting.

The app also uses **Firebase App Check** with **Google Play Integrity** to confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the app.

---

## Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by **Google Play**. We receive only whether you have an active subscription. We never receive or store your payment details.

---

## How We Use Information

Only to:

- provide the app’s features, synchronization and family sharing;
- generate AI insights when you ask for them;
- improve stability and understand feature usage in aggregate;
- deliver reminders and notifications you enabled.

We do **not** sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

---

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can:

- delete records or child profiles in the app; they are deleted from the cloud too, and from your family members’ devices;
- **delete your account** in **Settings → Account → Delete account**. This deletes your account, the children you own with all their records and photos from our cloud and from your device, and removes you from children others shared with you. Children you shared disappear for your family members too.
- ask us to delete your account by email if you no longer have the app: see [Delete your account](delete-account-android.md).

Deleted data is removed from our cloud immediately. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for a limited time under Firebase’s retention settings and are not linked to the content you entered.

You can export your records as a CSV file in **Settings → Export Data**.
